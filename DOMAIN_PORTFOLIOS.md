## Design principles

- Each page has its own professional headline, role targets, technical capabilities, selected work experience, and recruiter-oriented case studies.
- Cross-domain projects are reframed around the engineering value relevant to that portfolio rather than copied with identical descriptions.
- The existing general portfolio remains unchanged.
- Every page includes a **Save as PDF** action so a tailored portfolio can also be submitted as a PDF.
